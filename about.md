# Session Stats

A small overlay that tracks how your grinding is going **this session**:

- **Attempt** count
- **Deaths** this session (noclip "deaths" and GD's hidden anti-cheat spike are ignored)
- **Session Best** % (practice mode doesn't count)
- **Last Death** %
- **Time** spent actually playing (pause time excluded)
- **All-time Deaths** across every level since you installed the mod

You also get a **NEW BEST!** popup when you beat your session best, and a **GG!** popup with your attempt count when you finish the level.

Everything can be changed in the mod settings: corner, size, opacity, and whether popups show.
