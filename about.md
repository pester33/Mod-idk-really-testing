# IPGDGLL List

Browse the [IPGDGLL](https://ipgdgll.vercel.app) list without leaving Geometry Dash.

Open **Search** (the magnifying glass on the Create/Online screen) and press **IPGDGLL List**. You'll see every level on the list in order with its rank, creator and whether it's **Possible** or **Impossible**. Use the tabs to filter, and press **View** on any level to open it and play.

## Sending progress (optional)
Copy your **Mod token** from your profile on the site (in the *Geode mod* section) and paste it into this mod's settings. New bests on list levels are then sent as **pending** records for admins to review. Practice mode, start positions and platformer levels are never sent.
